Our History

Oasis of Wisdom Bible Church (OWBC) was established with a mandate to nurture leaders and build a spiritually vibrant community. Over the years, it has grown to become a significant spiritual family in Ilorin, Nigeria, inspiring lives locally and globally.

Our Mission

To develop spiritually mature and distinguished Christians who live impactful lives through personal transformation, biblical leadership, and consistent service to humanity and God.
